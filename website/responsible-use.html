Responsible Use & Academic Integrity Policy

Ghostline is an open-source assistive technology designed to empower learning, study comprehension, mock interview practice, and meeting productivity.

Intended & Authorized Use Cases

Interactive Study & Exam Preparation

Use Ghostline as an on-demand tutor while working through practice exams, sample certifications, and problem sets. Hover over complex questions to understand why an answer is correct and read detailed conceptual explanations.

Mock Interview Simulation

Practice for technical screening and behavioral interviews. Test how your saved candidate experience maps to common behavioral prompts and refine your spontaneous verbal answers.

Assistive Accessibility

Provides visual and cognitive support for users with motor difficulties, reading disabilities, or visual processing challenges by extracting text into high-contrast readable HUD overlays.

Meeting Note-Taking & Reference

Capture live meeting discussions locally, review complex technical discussions, and generate instant summaries without transmitting proprietary internal company discussions to third-party cloud services.

Academic Honesty & Prohibited Uses

Academic Integrity Notice: Ghostline is NOT a cheat tool. Ghostline must not be used to bypass proctored examination rules, violate institutional honor codes, or gain unauthorized advantages during real examinations or assessments.

Users of Ghostline must adhere to the following principles:

  • Institutional Compliance: Do not use Ghostline during proctored exams, quizzes, or certifications where external aids are prohibited by your educational institution or testing organization.
  • Professional Transparency: When engaging in formal workplace evaluations, respect your organization's policies regarding generative AI and assistive tools.
  • Respect Privacy of Third Parties: When capturing audio during meetings or calls, ensure all participants are aware of transcription if required by local consent laws (e.g., two-party consent jurisdictions).

Open Source & Transparency

Ghostline is distributed under the permissive MIT License. We believe in software that respects user privacy, operates under full user control, and leaves no digital surveillance footprint. As an open-source tool, users are ultimately responsible for complying with all applicable laws, employment agreements, and academic codes of conduct.
