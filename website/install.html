Installation & Setup Guide

Get Ghostline running on Windows in under 3 minutes with full offline AI inference or BYOK cloud connectivity.

System Prerequisites

  • Operating System: Windows 10 (Build 1903+) or Windows 11 64-bit
  • Processor: Modern 64-bit x86-64 Intel or AMD CPU (AVX2 supported)
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended for 7B models)
  • Graphics (Optional): NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060+ or AMD GPU for hardware acceleration
  • Storage: 1.5 GB free disk space (+ model files)
  • Audio: Standard audio input (microphone) & output (headphones/speakers)

Step 2

Install Ollama (For 100% Offline Inference)

Ollama powers local offline AI models with zero external internet traffic. Install via Windows Package Manager or official installer:

winget install Ollama.Ollama

Note: If you plan to use Cloud models (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or Anthropic), you can skip Ollama and input your API keys directly in Settings.

Step 3

Pull Your Recommended AI Model

Download your preferred local reasoning model. For quick question answering on standard laptops, we recommend Microsoft's phi3:mini (3.8B):

ollama pull phi3:mini

Step 4

Launch Ghostline & Test Screen Recognition

Double-click the Ghostline shortcut or Run-Ghostline.bat. Ghostline sits quietly in your system tray without interrupting other windows.

To test screen solving:

  1. Hover your mouse cursor over any question on your screen.
  2. Press Alt + /.
  3. The non-focus-stealing floating HUD will display the recognized question, answer, and confidence badge.
Step 5

Configure Audio Copilot & Personal Profile

For interviews, meetings, and classes, navigate to Settings:

  • My Profile: Upload your resume or write your key projects and skills. Ghostline will ground all interview answers in your authentic candidate voice.
  • Audio & Meetings: Verify your microphone and loopback audio device. Press Alt + A during calls for live speech transcription.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Ollama is installed but Ghostline shows "Daemon Unreachable"

Make sure the Ollama daemon is running. Check your Windows System Tray for the Ollama llama icon or run ollama serve in Command Prompt. Once running, Ghostline reconnects automatically.

Does Ghostline send my screen or voice recordings to any server?

In Local Mode (Ollama + Whisper), zero network calls are made. Screen OCR and speech transcription happen 100% on your machine. In BYOK Cloud Mode, prompts are sent strictly to your chosen provider via encrypted HTTPS.

How do I verify the integrity of my download?

Compare the SHA-256 hash in PowerShell using Get-FileHash Ghostline-Setup.exe against our published SHA256SUMS.txt on GitHub.
